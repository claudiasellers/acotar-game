# Place ambience loops (lane L32)

Self-made: synthesised from seeded noise and sine partials by
`tools/landscape/gen_place_audio.py` (no recordings, samples or downloads).
Licence: CC0 1.0. Rebuild with `python3 -I tools/landscape/gen_place_audio.py [name]`.
Played as positional loops at each place by `PlaceComposer` through
`data/places/sounds/<name>.tres` (SoundCue, Sound bus).

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| brook.ogg | water over stones: filtered rush and rising bubble blips |
| fire.ogg | bonfire roar and crackles |
| bees.ogg | a garden of bees, a few small birds |
| chimes.ogg | faerie glade: pentatonic bells over a soft pad |
| wind_birds.ogg | open hillside wind and bird phrases |
| bubbles.ogg | a cauldron's slow bubbling (the potion house) |
| mill.ogg | a water wheel: brook, wooden creak and paddle slap |
| village.ogg | murmur of voices, a far bell, birds |
| blight_drone.ogg | the blight: beating sub drone, sick air, groaning wood (horror, R3) |
| shrine_hum.ogg | old shrine: a quiet sung hum and a rare bell |
