# Faces: expression shapes and the expression system (lane L36)

Owner: lane L36 (facial expressions). Changing a name below needs the design director's OK (R13).

## 1. Shape keys (blend shapes)

Every cast head built on Feyre's kit has the **17 base keys** from
`tools/blender/feyre/build_feyre.py` (`nourished`, `blink_L`, `blink_R`,
`jaw_open`, `smile`, `frown`, `brow_up`, `brow_down`, `squint`, `cheek_puff`,
`lips_pucker`, `vis_AA`, `vis_E`, `vis_O`, `vis_U`, `vis_MBP`, `vis_FV`) and,
after `tools/blender/expressions/apply_face_keys.py <id>`, these **23 expression keys**
(`face_keys.EXPRESSION_KEYS`):

| Key | Move |
|---|---|
| `smile_L`, `smile_R` | one corner up, out and back, fold beside it (the smirk) |
| `frown_L`, `frown_R` | one corner down and tucked |
| `sneer_L`, `sneer_R` | upper lip and nose wing lift on one side |
| `lip_press` | lips thinned into a line |
| `jaw_clench` | jaw shut past rest, masseters bulge |
| `mouth_stretch` | fear grimace: corners wide, back and down |
| `eyes_wide_L`, `eyes_wide_R` | lids open past rest |
| `squint_L`, `squint_R` | one-sided narrowing |
| `brow_down_L/R`, `brow_up_L/R` | one-sided brows |
| `brow_inner_up` | worry, pleading |
| `cheek_raise` | the cheeks of a real smile |
| `mouth_side_L`, `mouth_side_R` | whole mouth shifted to one side |
| `chin_raise` | chin bunched, lower lip pushed up (holding back tears) |
| `lips_part` | lips apart with the jaw still (awe, fear, a snarl's bared teeth) |

**The mouth opens (L36-2).** `tools/blender/expressions/mouth.py` splits the lips along
the groove (the lip region is subdivided first), rolls each lip edge inward, and
carries the lower lip with the jaw in `jaw_open` and the jaw share of every
viseme. A new mesh **`Mouth`** in `<id>.glb` holds the mouth bag (deep rose,
never grey), an upper and lower row of pearly teeth and a tongue, skinned upper
half to `Head` and lower half to `Jaw`, with every face key (only the lower half
moves). Fangs per face in `apply_face_keys.TEETH` (Tamlin's, [C ch11]). The cut
is recorded on the body as the custom property `mouth_z` and the point attribute
`mouth_side` (+1 upper-lip seam copy, -1 lower).

`_L` is the character's own left (+X in Blender). Values are 0..1. The neutral
face (every key at 0) is unchanged by the pass. Landmarks are read from the base
keys, so the pass fits any face on the kit and is re-run after a face rebuild
(the sisters' and Feyre's new faces: run it on main once they merge). Meshes
that ride the face get the same keys: anything already carrying the base keys
(Lucien's `Scar`), plus `FACE_RIDERS` in the apply script (the father's beard in
`hair/father_greying.glb`, which now also follows `jaw_open` and the visemes).

## 2. Profiles: `data/faces/<id>_face.tres` (`FaceProfile`)

Generated by `python3 tools/blender/expressions/gen_face_profiles.py` from
`tools/blender/expressions/face_profiles.py` (edit the Python). A profile holds
the character's canon presets (shape-key mixes), its `rest` face, blink timing,
tics (Lucien's smirk, Alis's tongue click, Tamlin's jaw, the father's wince), and
`aliases`: every word of the shared emotion vocabulary maps to one of its own
presets.

Shared vocabulary (`FaceProfile.EMOTIONS`): `neutral happy laugh smirk tender
angry fury contempt disgust sad grief afraid terror surprise pained weary
guarded pleading`. A caller may also name a character's own preset (`restraint`,
`vicious_grin`, `tongue_click`, `dozing`, ...). Unknown names fall back to rest.

Spring Court folk (Tamlin, Lucien, Alis) wear fused masks over brows, nose and
cheeks [C ch6]; their presets act through jaw, mouth, lips and eyes. For a
profile with `masked = true` the driver never writes the brow and cheek keys
(`FaceDriver.MASK_HIDDEN`) and caps the squints at 0.6, so the rigid mask is never
pierced; `face_profiles.check()` also refuses those keys in masked presets. When a
mask comes off for good (after Amarantha) the profile's `masked` flips.

## 3. Asking for a face (story, dialogue, cutscenes)

Use the static helpers in `systems/faces/faces.gd`; never touch a FaceDriver:

```gdscript
Faces.express(actor: StringName, expression: StringName, intensity: float = 1.0, seconds: float = -1.0)
Faces.clear(actor: StringName)
Faces.speak(actor: StringName, text: String, emotion: StringName = &"", seconds: float = -1.0)
```

`actor` is the cast id or a story actor id (model in group `story_actor_<id>`).
`seconds < 0` holds until `clear`. `speak` mouths visemes from the text's letters
over `seconds` (default ~0.055 s per letter) and holds `emotion` for the line.

Transport (all EventBus):
- now: `story_actor_cue(actor, "face_<expression>")` and `"face_clear"`;
  cutscene tracks may emit these cues directly.
- requested from the story/core lane (R14), used automatically once present:
  `signal face_requested(actor: StringName, expression: StringName, intensity: float, seconds: float)`
  `signal line_spoken(actor: StringName, text: String, emotion: StringName, seconds: float)`
  Until `line_spoken` exists, `Faces.speak` reaches drivers through the scene-tree group `faces`.

## 4. The driver: `systems/faces/face_driver.gd` (`FaceDriver`)

Attached by `cast_model.gd` to every cast model with a profile. Layers: rest ->
expression (0.22 s in, 0.45 s out) -> tics -> visemes (talking eases off
`lip_press`/`jaw_clench`) -> blinks. Writes every face mesh under the model.
Dormant beyond 15 m from the camera (the body hands over to LOD1 at 14 m) or when
hidden. Contract test: `tests/unit/characters/test_faces.gd`.
