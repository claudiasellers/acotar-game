# Contract: creatures

Owner: creatures lane (`creatures/`, `data/creatures/`, `tools/blender/creatures.py`).

## Physics
- Creatures are `CharacterBody3D` on layer 4 (`1 << 3`, same as targets) so arrows and Feyre's aim ray hit them; mask = world (layer 1).
- Models face -Z with feet at y = 0. `CreatureData.model_scene` swaps the art in with no code change.

## Reads from other lanes
- Wind: first node in group `wind_source` with `wind_at(pos: Vector3) -> Vector3`; snowfall via `snowfall_at(pos) -> float` or property `snowfall` on the same node (0 if absent).
- Arrow type: `arrow.arrow_id` (StringName), else meta `arrow_id`, else `&"wood"`. `&"ash"` is lethal to fae.
- Player: group `player`; signals `bow_released(pos, loudness)` (optional), `arrow_fired(arrow)`.

## API
- `CreatureSenses.emit_noise(tree, pos, loudness)`: loudness 1.0 carries full hearing range.
- `Creature.on_arrow_hit(arrow)`, `Creature.die(cause)`, signals `wounded(cause)`, `died(cause)`. `scripted = true` pauses the brain.
- `Harvestable.harvest() -> Array[{item_id: StringName, qty: int}]` (once), `Harvestable.nearest(tree, pos, reach)`.
- `TrackLayer.leave(tree, kind, pos, heading, size)`; `marks_near(pos, radius)`.
- `GreatWolf`: `took_prey(prey)` (he never notices Feyre; `look_at_feyre` is ignored); export `kill_flag` (empty in the game; the story lane owns `wolf_killed`).
- `BeastActor`: `begin_approach(door)`, `break_door()`, `hold_presence()`, `loom(pos)`, `speak()`, `lead(pos)`, `withdraw()`, `pressure_at(pos) -> float` (0 beyond 18 m).
- `FamilyMember.set_scripted(bool)`, `go_to(pos)`.
- `StorySpawner` (add one to any scene hosting a story beat) answers `story_spawn_requested` for `great_wolf`, `doe`, `deer`, `hare`, `wolf`, `tamlin_beast`.

## EventBus (signals defined by the story lane; used only if present)
- Emits `creature_killed(creature_id, kind, cause, position)`. Kinds: `deer`, `hare`, `bird`, `wolf`, `great_wolf`. Cause: `arrow_<arrow_id>`; a creature that dies with an ash wound in it reports `arrow_ash` even if a wood arrow finished it.
- Emits `creature_harvested(creature_id, kind, items: PackedStringArray)`.
- Listens: `story_spawn_requested(spawn_id, transform)`, `story_actor_cue(actor, cue)`; beast cues `enter`, `loom`, `speak`, `lead`, `leave`; family cues `gather`, `release`.

## Spawning (Spring Court ambient life and every later roster)
- Tables: `data/creatures/spawn/<region_id>.tres` (`CreatureSpawnTable` of `SpawnEntry`), generated by `tools/creatures/gen_spawn_tables.py`. Entries carry scene, count, groups, cluster radius, active hours (start > end wraps midnight), required/forbidden flags, glamour gate (`ANY`, `GLAMOURED_ONLY`, `TRUE_FORM_ONLY`), `on_water`, and `canon`.
- Region lanes add one `CreatureSpawner` (`creatures/spawning/creature_spawner.gd`) to their region scene with `table`, `bounds` (world Rect2 x/z), and for ponds `water_level` + `water_bounds`. Heights come from `WorldHeight.height_at`. Placement is seeded (`seed`).
- Swarms (`AmbientSwarm`: butterflies by day, fireflies at dusk, faelights by night) are one MultiMesh each; nothing ambient is a per-instance node.
- Tables shipped: manor_grounds, border_woods, eastern_woods, starlight_glen, solstice_meadow, blossom_vale, rosewild; wider court: western_fells, fae_village, autumn_foothills, hill_of_the_rite, blighted_reaches (crows only).
- Ambient species scenes: `creatures/ambient/scenes/<name>.tscn` for songbird_blue/rose/gold, lark, hen, owl, hawk, crow, rabbit, lamb, sheep, fox, cat, squirrel, swan, butterflies, fireflies, faelights. `CreatureData.model_scale` lets one mesh serve a bigger cousin.

## Story actors (Spring Court)
`StorySpawner` answers `bogge`, `suriel`, `naga` (+ `naga_2`..), `attor`, `white_horse` (plus the M1 ids).
- Slice 3 ids: `puca` (cues `looks_like_father`, `shift_image`: father → bow → supplies → sisters, its own shape flickering between), `summer_faerie` (cue `dies`; signal `died`), `high_fae_male`, `_2`, `_3` (the three Calanmai lesser faeries; cue `flee`, then they vanish). - `BeastPatrol` (`creatures/beast/beast_patrol.tscn`): region lanes add one with Marker3D children as the route. Tamlin's beast walks it 21:00 to 04:00 under actor id `tamlin_beast_patrol` (story cues never reach it); `forbid_flags` takes him off duty.
- Glamour: `unseen_shimmer` entries (manor_grounds) are GLAMOURED_ONLY. Water sprites ride `on_water` in border_woods, eastern_woods, starlight_glen and the `court_waters` table (one spawner per lake). A numeric suffix spawns another of the base kind; the full id becomes the actor's `actor_id` / `creature_id`, so cues and kill reports address that one.
- `suriel` (Suriel): cues `caught`, `naga_are_coming` (scream, heard by every creature in range); signal `screamed`.
- `naga` (Naga, a Creature): lurks low; cue `attack` charges Feyre; signal `struck_player(naga)` and, if defined, `EventBus.player_struck(id, kind, amount)`. Two wood arrows kill; ash kills. Reports `creature_killed(id, &"naga", cause, pos)`.
- `bogge` (Bogge): no model. Cue `pass_behind` keeps it behind the camera for `pass_seconds`, darkening a FogVolume; signals `begged(line_id)` (`bogge_beg_1..4`), `passed`. Its `global_position` is what the gaze check tests.
- `attor` (Attor): model in a `GlamourLayer` TRUE_FORM child (invisible until `glamour_lifted`); cue `speaks_of_her`; signals `spoke`, `breathed(pos)` for audio.
- `white_horse` (WhiteHorse): `mount_point` Marker3D at 1.55 m, group `mount`; cues `walk`/`lead`, `stop`; `walk_to(pos)`.

## Special arrows (gameplay lane's Arrow.arrow_effect)
- `Creature.on_arrow_effect(effect, arrow)`: `sleep` drops it where it stands for 25 s (fae 5 s); `barbed` bleeds (blood trail) and slows to 55% for 15 s; `lure` draws every calm creature within 35 m to the arrow (`Creature.lure(tree, at)`; prey walk over, fleeing prey ignore it). Lure arrows that hit the ground work through the arrow's `hit` signal. Fire and frost are left to the gameplay lane's ElementState.
