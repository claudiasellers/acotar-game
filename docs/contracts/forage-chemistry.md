# Forage chemistry (lane L62)

Builds on L12's tag engine (`systems/chemistry/chemistry.gd`) and L59's
ElementField. Rules come from `lore/05-foraging-chemistry/tags-and-rules.md`:
same tags reinforce, opposites cancel, fae multiplies and tolls, bitter flips
healing to harm, bind for salves and steeps. L59's rule 3 holds: forageables
never react with each other in the world; they combine only in a pot at a
station, and what comes out reaches the world through an element.

## Data
- `data/chemistry/brew_rule.gd` (BrewRule): a named brew matched on what a
  result does (`need` key -> minimum over effect tags or heal / harm /
  fae_toll / hunger_relief; `methods`, `kind`, `forbid`, `priority`,
  `element`), with Feyre's note and one witness combination.
- `data/chemistry/brews/*.tres`: generated by `tools/chemistry/gen_brews.py`.
  Edit the table, never the .tres. Ingredient tags stay in
  `tools/gen_gameplay_data.py` (L12).

## Interfaces
- `Chemistry.combine()` results carry `brew` (BrewRule id or "") and
  `element` (fire, water or ""). A DishData name still wins over a brew name.
- `ProofJournal.brews` (saved); `record()` sets `result.new_brew`;
  signal `brew_discovered(id)`.
- `ChemistryStations.bind(node, station_id)`: ManorStation ids `hearth`,
  `kitchen`, `bread_oven`, `stillroom` and nodes in group `hearth` (the cottage)
  get a CookingStation child (unlit, not in group `station`).
- `BrewMenu.open_for(feyre, cooking_station)`; root node name `HearthPanel`.
- `BrewEffects.scent_scale(player)` (CreatureSenses scent range; beasts and
  lesser fae only, never High Fae, Keeper 2026-11-03).
- `BrewTells` on Feyre: scene-lit particles for bright (motes), warm (haze)
  and cool (rime). No lights and no EMISSION: a brew acts on her, nothing
  comes from her before she is Made (Keeper).
- `RecipeUnlocks` (L62-4): every recipe (27 brews + the DishData not
  already a brew's witness combo) with `needs`, `how_to()`, `hint()`.
  `CollectionBook.unlock_recipes()` runs on every `item_added` and marks a
  recipe found on the Recipes page the first time her bags hold all its
  needs; `brew_discovered` marks it too. FindCard shows "Recipe unlocked"
  with the bottle and how to make it; unlocks in one frame share one card.
- ArrowEffects: a fire arrow calls `ElementField.ignite_at(at, 1.2)`, a frost
  arrow `soak_at(at, 1.8)`.
